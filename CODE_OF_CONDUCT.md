# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want SleepPilot to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background or language. We pledge to make participation harassment-free and constructive.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and specific. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith; ask questions before assigning motives.
- Keep discussions on topic and avoid sharing other people’s private information.
- Respect the law and the rights of software authors: do not use the project to attack systems or distribute infringing material.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing private information (doxxing) or credentials.
- Encouraging illegal activity, including unauthorized access or circumvention of technical protection measures.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Scope

This applies in repository spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and in other public spaces when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and comments that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban participants. Reports can be sent privately to the maintainers through the repository’s contact/security channel. Reports will be handled confidentially and in good faith.

## Attribution

This is a short, project-specific code of conduct, informed by common open-source community standards such as the Contributor Covenant.
